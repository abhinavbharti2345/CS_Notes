# 📚 Canonical Computer Science & AI Reading List

> The timeless, gold-standard textbooks and engineering books recommended for deep conceptual mastery across CS fundamentals, distributed systems, and AI.

---

## 🏛️ 1. Core CS, Programming & Architecture
- **Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces (OSTEP)** — *Remzi Arpaci-Dusseau & Andrea Arpaci-Dusseau* (Free online, the single best OS textbook).
- **Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective (CS:APP)** — *Randal E. Bryant & David R. O'Hallaron* (Hardware, memory hierarchy, assembly, and linking).
- **Computer Networking: A Top-Down Approach** — *James Kurose & Keith Ross* (The gold standard for TCP/IP, sockets, and networking protocols).
- **Effective Java (3rd Edition)** — *Joshua Bloch* (Mandatory for writing idiomatic, robust, clean Java).

---

## 🏗️ 2. Distributed Systems & System Design
- **Designing Data-Intensive Applications (DDIA)** — *Martin Kleppmann* (The undisputed bible of distributed systems, replication, sharding, and consensus).
- **System Design Interview – An Insider's Guide (Vols 1 & 2)** — *Alex Xu & Sahn Lam* (Practical visual architecture patterns for real-world services).
- **Database Internals: A Deep Dive into How Distributed Data Systems Work** — *Alex Petrov* (B-Trees, LSM Trees, Storage engines, and distributed consensus).

---

## 🤖 3. Machine Learning, Deep Learning & AI Systems
- **Deep Learning** — *Ian Goodfellow, Yoshua Bengio, and Aaron Courville* (The MIT Deep Learning Book for mathematical fundamentals).
- **Hands-On Machine Learning with Scikit-Learn, Keras, and TensorFlow (3rd Ed)** — *Aurélien Géron* (The most practical, code-first ML guide).
- **Programming Massively Parallel Processors (PMPP)** — *David B. Kirk & Wen-mei W. Hwu* (The definitive textbook for GPU computing and CUDA architecture).
