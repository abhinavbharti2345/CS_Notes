## 🏛️ University Lecture Series (Free & Public)
- **MIT 6.824: Distributed Systems** — *Prof. Robert Morris* (Raft consensus, MapReduce, Spanner, Zookeeper; labs in Go).
- **CMU 15-445/645: Database Systems** — *Prof. Andy Pavlo* (Buffer pools, B+ Trees, Concurrency control, Query execution).
- **Stanford CS229: Machine Learning** — *Andrew Ng* (Linear models, SVMs, kernels, statistical learning).
- **Stanford CS231n: Deep Learning for Computer Vision** — *Andrej Karpathy & Justin Johnson* (Backpropagation, CNNs, optimization).
- **Andrej Karpathy's "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero"** (Building micrograd, makemore, GPT-2 from scratch in pure Python/PyTorch).
- **MIT 6.5940: TinyML and Efficient Deep Learning Computing** — *Prof. Song Han* (Quantization, pruning, efficient LLM inference, FlashAttention).
